DicodePing Fox uses the Firefox Accounts and Guardian authentication protocol described in FoxyVPN by Vauth (MIT). The production relay embeds the HTTP transport from Vauth/sing-box v1.0.0-masque, commit 9be46c8d5c37b5832a879ec78b2c8d75f3286c20 (GPL-3.0-or-later), used by FoxyVPN 2.0.0. The pinned source and checksums are recorded in go.mod/go.sum. Both Android and desktop build this helper from source. HTTP/2, uTLS Chrome fingerprinting and TLS record fragmentation are enabled; HTTP/3 is not enabled because raw UDP physical-interface protection is not yet implemented in the helper. The legacy relay remains only for regression comparison. This is an unofficial client, not endorsed by Mozilla. Account eligibility, verification and bandwidth limits are enforced by the provider. No passwords are retained and no account or CAPTCHA verification is bypassed.
