DicodePing Fox uses the Firefox Accounts, Guardian and HTTP/2 CONNECT protocol described in FoxyVPN v1.0.7 by Vauth (MIT). Authentication and challenge handling are adapted from that project; its license accompanies every helper package. This is an unofficial client, not endorsed by Mozilla. Account eligibility, verification and bandwidth limits are enforced by the provider. No passwords are retained and no account or CAPTCHA verification is bypassed.
